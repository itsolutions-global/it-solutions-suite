Why Sending Prompt Meeting Recaps Multiplies Project Success

Over 60% of client project disputes and missed deadlines stem from unrecorded verbal agreements. When you send a crisp, written recap email within two hours of a call, you establish an undisputed paper trail, assign accountability, and reassure clients that their budget is in capable hands. EchoStream automates this entire discipline in seconds without risking client privacy.

1. Stop Scope Creep Before It Starts

Clients frequently remember casual brainstorming as committed deliverables. EchoStream segregates "Decisions Approved" from "Open Questions", ensuring new features require formal scope adjustments.

2. Explicit Task Owners & Deadlines

A task without an owner is a task that never gets completed. EchoStream extracts individual speaker commitments and tags them with clear checkboxes `[ ] [Name]` and due dates.

3. Built-in VoiceGuard Credential Stripping

Teams often paste API keys, database credentials, or staging passwords into chat logs during calls. EchoStream automatically masks credentials (`[REDACTED_CREDENTIAL]`) before producing your email.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About EchoStream

Does EchoStream record or upload my meeting audio or text?

No. EchoStream does not access your microphone, record audio, or send pasted text to any cloud servers. Everything operates 100% locally in your web browser. Your client conversations and internal discussions remain strictly confidential on your physical device.

Can I use transcripts from Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or Otter?

Yes. You can copy the transcript output from any meeting software (Zoom AI Companion, Teams transcript, Google Meet captions, Otter.ai, or Whisper) and paste it directly into EchoStream. The engine automatically parses speaker identifiers and extracts key points.

How does the built-in VoiceGuard sanitizer protect my passwords and keys?

Before analyzing meeting notes, EchoStream’s client-side sanitizer runs regex scans for AWS keys, OpenAI API keys, Stripe tokens, GitHub Personal Access Tokens, and database connection URIs. Any matched secrets are replaced with [REDACTED_CREDENTIAL] so they are never accidentally included in the email draft you send to clients.

Can I directly open the generated draft in Outlook, Gmail, or Apple Mail?

Yes. When you click "📧 Open in Mail Client", EchoStream creates a standardized mailto: link that opens your default email client with the Subject line and Body pre-filled and ready to send.

Is EchoStream free?

Yes. EchoStream is completely free, unmetered, and open-source under the MIT license. There are no monthly usage limits, no credit balances, and no paid upgrades.
