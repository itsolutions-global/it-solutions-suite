Zero Cloud Risk

Why Never Upload Client NDAs or Contracts to Cloud AI

Standard cloud-based contract tools and general LLMs ingest your sensitive contract text onto remote third-party servers, where it can be logged, inspected by platform operators, or stored in training databases. This frequently violates the very confidentiality terms you are reviewing. ClauseGuard solves this fundamentally: the entire rule engine executes inside your browser's V8 or JavaScriptCore runtime using pure client-side regex heuristics. Your agreement never leaves your device.

1. Uncapped Financial Liability

A client contract without a liability cap puts your personal assets, bank accounts, and business at risk for indirect or consequential damages. ClauseGuard identifies missing liability caps and supplies standard "aggregate fees paid" limitation clauses.

2. Pre-Existing IP Assignment Overreach

Predatory "Work for Hire" clauses often claim ownership not just of project deliverables, but of your prior inventions, background libraries, and software tools. ClauseGuard flags broad IP grabs and inserts clear background IP reservation carveouts.

3. Extended Payment & Cancellation Traps

Clauses like "Net 90 days", "Pay-When-Paid", and unilateral termination without paying for work-in-progress starve freelancers of cash flow. ClauseGuard replaces these with industry-standard Net-15 or Net-30 payment guarantees.

Frequently Asked Questions About ClauseGuard

Does ClauseGuard upload my contract or NDA to any cloud server?

No, absolutely not. ClauseGuard runs 100% locally in your web browser. When you paste or drop a contract file, the text is evaluated solely within your browser tab’s memory. No HTTP POST requests are dispatched, no analytics track your text, and no third-party APIs are called.

You can verify this yourself by opening your browser's Developer Tools (Network tab) and hitting "Scan Contract for Risks" while offline.

What specific contract clauses does ClauseGuard scan for?

ClauseGuard scans for ten of the most damaging contractual traps encountered by freelancers, agencies, and small businesses:

  • Uncapped Liability: Phrases indicating unlimited damages or absence of a liability ceiling.
  • Pre-Existing IP Forfeiture: Clauses attempting to seize background tools, reusable code, or prior inventions.
  • Broad Non-Competes: Clauses restricting your ability to work with other clients in your industry.
  • Evergreen Auto-Renewals: Traps locking you into recurring commitments with unreasonable cancellation windows.
  • Delayed Payment Terms: Net-60, Net-90, or conditional "pay-when-paid" clauses.
  • Unilateral Termination: Client rights to cancel without compensating you for work performed to date.
  • One-Sided Indemnification: Requiring you to indemnify the client without reciprocal protection.
  • Hostile Jurisdiction / Foreign Arbitration: Forcing legal disputes into distant or expensive venues.
  • Perpetual NDA Survival: Overly broad non-disclosure obligations extending indefinitely without trade-secret limits.
  • Unilateral Contract Amendments: Reserving the right to change terms without mutual written agreement.
Can I download or copy the redlines for my negotiation?

Yes. With one click on "📋 Copy Redlines", all flagged issues and their counter-proposals are copied to your clipboard formatted in clean markdown. You can also click "📥 Download Report" to save an offline text summary for your legal archives or to attach to an email back to the client.

Is ClauseGuard a substitute for licensed legal counsel?

ClauseGuard is an automated heuristic auditing and drafting aid designed to flag common business traps and provide balanced commercial drafting options. It does not constitute formal legal advice. For high-stakes, multi-million dollar or specialized transactions, we always recommend consulting a qualified attorney licensed in your jurisdiction.

Yes. ClauseGuard is 100% free and unmetered. There are no paywalls, no monthly document quotas, no credit systems, and no account creation requirements. It is provided freely by IT Solutions Global as part of our commitment to uncompromising utility and user trust.
