Statutory Reference Guide: MSMED Act Section 15, 16, 23 & Income Tax Section 43B(h)

1. Maximum Credit Window (Section 15): Even if a purchase order states Net-60 or Net-90 days, Section 15 caps the maximum legal credit period for registered Micro and Small Enterprises at 45 days (or 15 days where no written credit term exists).

2. Monthly-Rests Compounding (Section 16): Unlike simple commercial interest, unpaid interest at 3x RBI Bank Rate compounds at the end of every month from the appointed day.
